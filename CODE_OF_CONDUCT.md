# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or
propose a change without being made to feel small for it. Many people using this project
are first-time sellers and first-time contributors. Treat them accordingly.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and be kind. Both, not one or the other.
- Critique the code, the data or the reasoning — not the person.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
- Explain your reasoning, especially when disagreeing.
- Remember that "obvious" usually means "familiar to you".

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults or demeaning comments
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Suriya-Ravichandran/amazon-india-seller-mcp/issues)
(or privately, if the report itself is sensitive). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide or reject contributions that breach this code, and may ban
anyone for repeated or serious violations.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commits — and
when representing the project publicly.
